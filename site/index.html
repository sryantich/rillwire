03 / THE ROAD AHEAD

Small releases.
Clear milestones.

Useful engineering starts now.
Live Discord compatibility needs a supported access decision.

  1. M0

    Research & native foundation NOW

    Offline desktop, fixture replay, cache limits, Windows measurement tools and branding.

  2. M1

    The text workflow

    Authorized access, history, delivery states, notifications and accessible interaction.

  3. M2

    Voice, with care

    DAVE encryption, audio devices, loss recovery and a dependable push-to-talk experience.

  4. M3

    A wider horizon

    Richer media, screen sharing and additional platforms, each measured on its own terms.
